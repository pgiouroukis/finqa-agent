Here is a template you can use for your final report. 

Introduction
A. Background and Motivation
B. Problem Statement
C. Objectives and Scope
II. Literature Review
A. Review of Relevant Literature 
B. Identification of Gaps in Existing Research (Optional)
III. Methodology
A. Data Collection and Preprocessing if any done
B. Model Selection
C. Optimization Procedure(s). Are you optimizing training or inference or both
D. Profiling tools and methods
E. Evaluation Metrics
IV. Experimental Results
A. Experimental Setup
B. Performance Comparison (before and after optimizations)of Different Models D. Analysis of Results
V. Discussion
A. Interpretation of Results
B. Comparison with Previous Studies
C. Challenges and Limitations 
D. Future Directions
VI. Conclusion
A. Summary of Findings
B. Contributions 
C. Recommendations for Future Research
VII. References A. List of Citations and Sources Used (including papers and source code)
